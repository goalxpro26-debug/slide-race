How to Play

  • Steer: drag, move the mouse, or use ← / → (A / D) to slide left and right down the water slide.
  • Wall ride: hug the slide wall for a speed boost — spray flies and you go faster.
  • Shove: steer into a rival to push them sideways. Shove one clean off the edge and they tumble behind you. +50 pts.
  • Shortcut: fall off the edge and you land on a lower section, skipping ahead! Risky — you lose speed and get stunned. (Drag past the edge to risk it; keyboard steering stays on the slide.)
  • Starfish: grab the orange starfish on the slide for +100 pts.
  • Finish in the top 3 to unlock the next race. 1st = 3 stars, 2nd = 2, 3rd = 1. You get 3 attempts per race.
  • Later races are twistier, narrower, and packed with more rivals.
